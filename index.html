Short Bio

Zhang Yuanzheng is a Senior Algorithm Engineer with over 7 years of experience in building large-scale recommendation systems and deep learning applications. He is currently at Alibaba (Amap), where he leads the development of AI-powered driving guidance broadcasting systems using reinforcement learning with human feedback (RLHF). Previously at VIVO, he drove the full algorithm iteration cycle from LR to deep learning for the App Store's recommendation system, serving hundreds of millions of users. He also created the open-source project GERBIL, a production-grade recommendation system covering data engineering, model training, and online serving. He holds a Master's degree from Beijing University of Posts and Telecommunications.
