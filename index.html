Short Bio

Zhang Da is a Senior Algorithm Engineer with 7 years of experience in recommendation and search systems. At Alibaba (Amap), he worked on AI-driven driving navigation broadcasting, leading deep-learning deployment for multi-task modeling, sequential modeling, and end-cloud co-design, and built the NIQ experience metric from zero to one. Previously at VIVO, he led the evolution of the App Store recommendation system through multiple generations of models — from LR to deep learning — covering retrieval, ranking, re-ranking, and real-time data infrastructure. He also created the open-source project GERBIL, a full-pipeline production-grade recommendation system. He holds a Master's degree in Electronic Science and Technology from Beijing University of Posts and Telecommunications, with two IEEE conference papers to his name.
